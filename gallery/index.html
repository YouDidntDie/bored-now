THE CABINET

Artifacts recovered from the history of games.

Each era of the tour leaves one collectible behind — all twenty-three, 1972 to 2017.

ARTIFACT 001

THE FIRST PADDLE

ERA 1972 · FROM PONG

The instrument of the first rally — table tennis on a television screen, 1972. Two dials, one square ball, and the object that started everything. Cast in brass, it has not moved since the last point was scored. Some say the ball is still in play.

ARTIFACT 002

STARSHARD

ERA 1978 · FROM STARFALL

A crystallized fragment of a shattered invader, recovered from the orbital defense of 1978. Every kill in the defense fell to earth in fragments — this is one of them, kept. Its cracks still hold the red glow of the swarm's last descent. Handle with care: it remembers falling.

ARTIFACT 003

THE SPLIT STONE

ERA 1979 · FROM FIELD

The first rock ever broken on purpose. In 1979 the pilots learned that a big rock is just two medium rocks waiting to happen — and the belt learned to fear the little triangle. Its heart still glows amber from the impact. It has never fit back together.

ARTIFACT 004

THE LAST PELLET

ERA 1980 · FROM HAUNT

The final orb of the 1980 maze, never eaten. When the pack learned to hunt as one, the eater's last hope was this glowing pellet — cracked the night the ghosts stopped running. It still leaks a little light. The maze remembers who was afraid.

ARTIFACT 005

THE FIRST BARREL

ERA 1981 · FROM APEX

The first barrel ever thrown in anger. In 1981 something big climbed to the top of a steel tower and started rolling — and climbers have been jumping ever since. Dented on impact, retired undefeated. It never missed twice.

ARTIFACT 006

THE LINE

ERA 1984 · FROM GARBAGE

Four blocks, one perfect row. In 1984 the wells kept filling and the only way out was through — four at once, the legendary BREAK, the move that buried a thousand opponents. Fused forever in the moment of the clear. Some rows never fall.

ARTIFACT 007

THE LAST BRICK

ERA 1976 · FROM SHATTER

The final brick of the 1976 wall, never broken. Paddles came and went, balls were lost by the dozen, but this one held its row through every level. Its crack runs the full length — proof it was hit, and proof it refused. Some walls outlast their players.

ARTIFACT 008

THE UNTAKEN

ERA 1980 · FROM ABDUCT

The one human the landers never got. In 1980 the sky was full of ships and the ground was full of running — but this figure stayed planted on the orange soil, unlifted, unconverted. Still standing. Still not a mutant.

ARTIFACT 009

THE LAST INTERCEPT

ERA 1980 · FROM RAIN

The final counter-missile of the 1980 defense, never fired. When the rain came down over the cities, this one stayed in its silo — the warhead it was meant for never made it through. Scorched at the tip from a near miss that never happened. It is still waiting for its war.

ARTIFACT 010

THE OPEN LANE

ERA 1981 · FROM TRAFFIC

The one lane nobody touched. In 1981 the highway was a wall of headlights and everybody wove — except here, where the asphalt stayed empty all night. Drivers swear it was luck. The lane keeps no records.

ARTIFACT 011

THE FIRST TUNNEL

ERA 1982 · FROM BURROW

A core sample of the 1982 dig, tunnel and all. The digger came through here first — one clean bore through dark soil, the walls still glowing from the pass. Every tunnel since has been a copy. The dirt remembers the way.

ARTIFACT 012

THE CHECKERED FLAG

ERA 1982 · FROM SLIPSTREAM

The first flag ever waved at the sunset circuit, 1982. It has seen eight cars become one winner, three laps become a legend, and more photo finishes than the judges admit. Tattered at the edge from being waved too hard. It never waved for second place.

ARTIFACT 013

THE WORN BUTTONS

ERA 1983 · FROM TRACK

Two arcade buttons from the 1983 circuit, worn smooth by a thousand thumbs. Sprinters mashed them in alternating rhythm until the plastic gave up its texture — the original speedrun controllers. They have nothing left to give, and they would do it again.

ARTIFACT 014

THE FIRST FLAG

ERA 1985 · FROM STOMP

The pennant from the end of stage one, 1985. Runners crossed pits and stomped walkers for this — a little triangle of cloth on a pole, the first finish line of the new school. Every flag since is a tribute act.

ARTIFACT 015

THE FINAL BELL

ERA 1991 · FROM CLASH

The ring bell from the 1991 circuit, cracked on the last strike. It ended a thousand rounds and one legendary final — the crack appeared the night nobody went down. It rang once for the winner and never again.

ARTIFACT 016

THE SPENT SHELL

ERA 1993 · FROM FRAG

A single shell from the 1993 campaign, fired at the base's front door. The marine walked in with a pump and a prayer; this is what the prayer sounded like. The demons heard it coming.

ARTIFACT 017

THE UNTHROWN SPHERE

ERA 1996 · FROM TAMER

A capture sphere from 1996, never thrown. Tamers carried these into every tall-grass rumor and every ladder match — this one stayed in the pocket, pristine, its catch still theoretical. Somewhere out there is the one that got away.

ARTIFACT 018

THE FIRST CRYSTAL

ERA 1998 · FROM COMMAND

The first crystal ever harvested, 1998. Three harvesters, one field, sixty an hour — and this shard started the economy that built a thousand bases. It still glows the color of money. Everything was built on this.

ARTIFACT 019

THE FADED POSTER

ERA 2001 · FROM WANTED

A wanted poster from 2001, faded past reading. The posse rode out for this face and the outlaw rode faster — by the time the ink dried, both were legends. The reward was never claimed.

ARTIFACT 020

THE FUSED PAIR

ERA 2001 · FROM SWAP

Two gems fused mid-swap, 2001. A violet diamond and a teal orb, locked at a single facet in the moment of the perfect exchange — the move that sent the junk and won the board. They have never been separated.

ARTIFACT 021

THE BROKEN STICK

ERA 2005 · FROM ENCORE

A drumstick snapped mid-solo, 2005. The chart hit its fastest section, the crowd hit its loudest cheer, and the stick hit its limit — right on the downbeat of an S rank. The show went on without it.

ARTIFACT 022

THE LAST BLOCK

ERA 2009 · FROM SIEGE

The final fortress block, still standing, 2009. Five shots came for the crystal and the walls fell one by one — except this one, scorched and upright in the rubble. It did not fall. The fortress did.

ARTIFACT 023

THE LAST RING

ERA 2017 · FROM DROP

The final circle of the 2017 drop, frozen at its smallest. Ninety-nine went in; the ring shrank and shrank until only one player stood inside it. The zone closed. The legend didn't.
